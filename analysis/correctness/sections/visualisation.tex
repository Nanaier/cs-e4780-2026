\subsection{Dashboard}
\label{sec:dashboard}

The results are meant for traders, so we built a dashboard in the style of a market data terminal that answers ``which of my symbols is trending, and since when?'' at a glance (Figure~\ref{fig:dashboard}).
\texttt{trading/viz.py} turns the \texttt{ema.csv} and \texttt{advisories.csv} of any engine into one self-contained HTML page.
A play button and a time slider replay the data hour by hour, and every window shows the state the live system had at that moment.
A watchlist table shows, for each symbol, its last close, change, both EMAs, their gap as a bar and a sparkline, its buy and sell counts and its latest advisory; it can be filtered by exchange, security type and trend, searched by symbol or ISIN, and sorted by any column.
The main panel plots the closing price per window with both EMAs and the advisories, with a read-out that follows the cursor; below it, the gap $\mathrm{EMA}_{38}-\mathrm{EMA}_{100}$ in percent of $\mathrm{EMA}_{100}$ is shaded green above zero and red below, so every advisory is a zero crossing.
Side windows give the selected symbol's key data, signal counts, trend share and crossovers per day, and the market as a whole: a signal feed, signals per day and per exchange, a 24-hour signal clock, flips against activity for every symbol, and the real trades per minute of the week (Section~\ref{sec:dataset}).
First-window advisories (Section~\ref{sec:design-space}) are hidden by default and shown as grey warm-up markers on request.
