\subsection{Dashboard}
\label{sec:dashboard}

The results are meant for traders, so we built a dashboard in the style of a market data terminal that answers ``which of my symbols is trending, and since when?'' at a glance (Figure~\ref{fig:dashboard}).
\texttt{trading/viz.py} turns the \texttt{ema.csv} and \texttt{advisories.csv} of any engine into one self-contained HTML page.
A watchlist table shows, for each symbol, its last close, both EMAs, their gap as a bar, its buy and sell counts and its latest advisory; it can be filtered by exchange, security type and trend, searched by symbol or ISIN, and sorted by any column.
The main panel plots the closing price per window with both EMAs and the advisories as markers, with a read-out that follows the cursor.
Below it, a trend panel shows the gap $\mathrm{EMA}_{38}-\mathrm{EMA}_{100}$ in percent of $\mathrm{EMA}_{100}$, shaded green above zero and red below, so that every advisory is visible as a zero crossing.
Side panels give the selected symbol's key data (exchange, ISIN, currency), its signal counts, its crossovers per day and its advisories.
A market-breadth histogram counts buy minus sell advisories per hour over the symbols that match the filters, next to a tape of their latest advisories.
First-window advisories (Section~\ref{sec:design-space}) are hidden by default and shown as grey warm-up markers on request, and the price axis follows the closing prices so that the EMA warm-up from zero does not flatten the chart.
